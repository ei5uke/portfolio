Time machines and AI are probably the two most famous tropes used in sci-fi today. Steins Gate 0 is an anime that combines the two innovations together directly in somewhat of a feedback loop. Herein, the creation of the AI speeds up the development of the time machine, and the creation of the time machine is what originally prompts the research of AI. If this chicken-and-egg scenario makes no sense, that's precisely because this features confusing time machine logic present in every time machine fiction piece ever.
